SID Player Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

SID Player is a Commodore 64 music player for Mac, iPhone and iPad, developed by Stuart Clark. It works entirely on your device. It doesn't collect, transmit, share or sell any personal information, and it has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.

What SID Player stores, and where

When you import tunes, SID Player copies them into its own storage on your device. On iPhone and iPad they are kept in the app's Documents folder, which you can see in the Files app. SID Player also saves a library index on your device. The index records each tune's details (such as title, composer and release information), when you added it, your favourites, play counts and when you last played it.

Your playlists, settings, any song-length (Songlengths.md5) or STIL databases you import, and corrections you make to generated scores are also saved on your device. None of this is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Network access

SID Player doesn't connect to the internet. It doesn't download tunes, artwork or metadata. Links in the app, such as to the High Voltage SID Collection, open in your web browser, and those websites handle your visit under their own policies.

Files you choose

SID Player only reads the files and folders that you select to import. It doesn't keep access to their original location after it has copied them. When you export a score as MusicXML or MIDI, you choose where to save it, and any storage or sharing service you choose handles that copy under its own policies.

System features

While music is playing, SID Player shares the tune's title, composer and progress with your device so they can appear on the Lock Screen, in Control Center and on connected accessories. Your device handles this information.

Your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup or Time Machine) may include SID Player's data according to your settings. On iPhone and iPad, files in the SID Player folder may also be visible to other apps you use with the Files app.

Children

SID Player doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your information

You can remove individual tunes or playlists, or use Settings > Remove All Tunes. Deleting SID Player removes its data from that device. Copies in device backups or exported files remain until you delete them.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use them only to respond to your request. You can contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of information you have sent us.

This website

This policy and the support page are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address, when you visit. See GitHub's privacy statement. These pages contain no added analytics, advertising, external fonts or tracking scripts.
