SID Player Support

Help with playing and organising your Commodore 64 music.

Email Stuart Clark at trautskralc@gmail.com.

When asking for help

Include your device model, operating system version, SID Player version and the steps that led to the problem. If a particular tune misbehaves, tell us its title, composer and, for tunes from the High Voltage SID Collection, its path in the collection. Please don't send passwords or other personal information.

Getting tunes

SID Player doesn't include or download any music. A large, free archive of C64 music is available from the High Voltage SID Collection (HVSC). Download and unzip it, then import the files or folders you want.

Importing tunes

On a Mac, choose Import and select .sid files or whole folders. On iPhone and iPad, choose Import, or copy tunes into the SID Player folder in the Files app; the library updates when you return to the app. Imported tunes are copied into SID Player, so you can delete or move the originals afterwards.

Tune lengths and subtune names

Import HVSC's DOCUMENTS/Songlengths.md5 and DOCUMENTS/STIL.txt in Settings, or import an HVSC folder that contains its DOCUMENTS folder. Without a song-length database, tunes play for the default length chosen in Settings.

A tune won't import or sounds wrong

SID Player plays PSID and RSID tunes. Damaged files, and tunes that need a MUS player or Commodore BASIC, are skipped. Some tunes were written for a particular SID chip: try changing the SID chip setting in Settings to MOS 6581 or MOS 8580, or back to Automatic.

No sound

Check the volume and the selected output device. If playback stops after a call or when headphones are disconnected, press Play to resume.

Support purchases

The optional purchases in Settings > Support the Developer are a way to say thank you. They unlock no features and can be bought more than once. For refunds or payment problems, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Privacy

Read the SID Player Privacy Policy. In short: SID Player keeps your library on your device and collects nothing.
